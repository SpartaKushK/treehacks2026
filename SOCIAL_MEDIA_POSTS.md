## 💡 Pro Tips for Virality

1. **Lead with fear, end with hope**: Health scares are shareable
2. **Make it personal**: "This could be you" hits harder than statistics
3. **Show, don't tell**: Demo videos > descriptions
4. **Lower the barrier**: "Text Poke 'CareSync'" is easier than "Download app, sign up..."
5. **Create FOMO**: "Caught health issue 3 days early" makes people think "what am I missing?"
6. **Incentivize shares**: "$1 per signup" means advocates benefit from sharing
7. **User-generated content**: Every user success story is free marketing
